Email

Professional business email powered by Microsoft 365 & Google Workspace.

We set up, migrate, and manage professional business email using Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, giving your team secure, reliable communication with custom domains, advanced security, and seamless collaboration tools.

Email is still the backbone of business communication, and getting it right means more than just creating mailboxes. For the companies we work with across MENA, the Gulf and Africa, email management covers identity, security, deliverability, archiving, mobile access and compliance, all of which need to work together quietly in the background.

We deploy and manage business email on Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, including custom domains, advanced security policies (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, anti-phishing, anti-spam), shared mailboxes, distribution lists, mobile and desktop client setup, and disaster-recovery archiving. For migrations from on-premise Exchange, cPanel email or another cloud platform, we run zero-downtime cut-overs with parallel mail flow until users are fully on the new system.

Once deployment is done, we keep things running. Licensing and user lifecycle (joiner-mover-leaver) is automated where possible, security policies are reviewed quarterly against new threats, and our helpdesk handles day-to-day issues, password resets, mobile setup, mailbox quotas, delegation, recovery of deleted mail. Your team gets fast support; you get a single monthly report covering volume, security incidents and policy posture.

Frequently asked questions

Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, which should we use?

Both are excellent. Microsoft 365 is usually the right choice for businesses already on Office, Teams or Active Directory. Google Workspace fits teams that prefer simpler administration, lighter-weight collaboration, and a strong web-first experience. We recommend in writing during discovery.

Will email be down during migration?

No. We run parallel mail-flow during cut-over so messages are delivered to both old and new systems for a configurable window. Users keep working throughout, and we cut DNS over only when the new system has been validated end-to-end.

How do you stop phishing and impersonation?

Through layered controls: SPF, DKIM and DMARC properly configured, advanced anti-phishing policies, attachment sandboxing, link rewriting, impersonation protection on executive accounts, and quarterly user training. Together these block 99%+ of inbound threats.

Can you help with email archiving and compliance?

Yes. We configure long-term retention, legal hold and eDiscovery for industries that need it, finance, legal, healthcare. Archive search is available to authorised admins through the standard Microsoft or Google compliance portals.

What about leavers, how do you handle their mailboxes?

Standard practice is to convert the leaver to a shared mailbox or archive licence so the data is preserved for legal and operational continuity, redirect inbound mail, and revoke device access. Our offboarding runbook covers all of this.
