Remote Team Management App

Stay in control of your remote team.

Manage your remote workforce with a system designed for visibility, structure, and efficiency. Keep track of performance, stay up to date on progress, and ensure your team stays aligned no matter where they are.

Managing a distributed team is now table-stakes for businesses across MENA, the Gulf and Africa, but most companies still run on a patchwork of WhatsApp groups, Excel timesheets and trust. The result is opacity: leadership cannot see what is being worked on, managers chase status updates, and high performers go unnoticed while issues build up quietly.

The Aligned remote-team platform replaces that with a single source of truth for distributed work. Each team member has a real-time activity view that captures working hours, active applications and task progress without invasive screen recording. Managers see live dashboards by department, project or individual; leadership sees aggregate KPIs by week or month. Tasks, milestones and approvals flow through the same platform so nothing slips between WhatsApp threads.

The platform is configurable to match how each business actually operates. Permission rules control who can see what, frontline managers see their team only, department heads see roll-ups, executives see the full picture. Reports can be exported to Excel or pushed into your existing payroll, HR or BI systems. Onboarding takes a few days, training takes an hour per role, and most teams reach steady-state in two weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Is this employee surveillance?

No. The platform tracks activity at a workflow level, tasks, working hours, project progress, not keystrokes or screen recordings. The goal is visibility for both sides: managers see progress, employees see their own contribution clearly tracked.

How long does it take to roll out?

For a team of 20-100 people, typically 5-10 business days. That includes configuration, integration with your existing tools, training sessions per role, and a two-week stabilisation period where we are on hand for adjustments.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The platform has native iOS and Android apps in addition to the web interface, so field teams, drivers and remote workers can update tasks, log hours and view their dashboard from anywhere.

Can we integrate it with our payroll or HR system?

Yes. We export to CSV, integrate via REST API, and have ready-made connectors for common payroll and HR platforms. Hours and absences flow through automatically so payroll runs without re-keying.

What happens to our data?

It stays yours. Data is hosted in the region you specify, encrypted at rest and in transit, and exportable at any time. We are SOC 2 aligned and offer custom data-residency options for regulated industries.
