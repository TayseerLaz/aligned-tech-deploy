Launch powerful WhatsApp campaigns through a Meta-verified setup with personalization and real engagement. From promotions and reminders to customer updates, we help you deliver the right message at the right time, directly where your audience is most active, without risking your number getting banned.

WhatsApp broadcasting is the highest-engagement marketing channel available, open rates regularly above 90%, click-through rates 5-10x higher than email, and message-read times measured in minutes, not hours. The catch is that doing it the wrong way, on a personal or unverified number, gets the number banned within days. We run WhatsApp campaigns the right way: through the official WhatsApp Business API, on Meta-verified numbers, with Meta-approved templates.

Every broadcast campaign starts with strategy. We help you segment your contact list, design the message templates, get them through Meta approval, and schedule sends at the right time for your audience. Personalisation is dynamic: each message can include the customer’s name, order details, account balance, appointment time or any field from your CRM. Delivery, read and reply rates are tracked in real time so you can measure performance and iterate.

We also handle the heavier lifting around Meta verification, getting your business profile approved with the green tick, configuring sender display names, managing the rolling rate limits Meta applies to new accounts, and warming up your number so deliverability stays high. For high-volume senders we set up multi-number rotation and quality monitoring so a single message never tanks your overall sender reputation.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from group messages or sending from my phone?

Group messages and personal-account broadcasts are against WhatsApp policy, they get the number banned, often within hours. Our setup uses the official Business API with approved templates, which is allowed at scale and protected from bans when used correctly.

Do I need Meta verification?

For best deliverability, yes, and we handle the entire process. Verification gives you the green-tick badge, higher message limits, and stronger trust signals. Without it the API still works but rate limits are tighter.

What kind of content can I send?

Promotional, transactional and utility messages, order updates, appointment reminders, payment confirmations, marketing campaigns. Each message uses a template that Meta has pre-approved. We design templates with you and submit them for approval.

How do you handle opt-in and unsubscribe?

Every campaign respects WhatsApp opt-in rules. We collect explicit opt-ins via web forms, in-store sign-ups or imported consent records, and every broadcast includes a clear opt-out path. This protects your sender reputation and keeps you compliant with regional privacy laws.

How much does it cost?

WhatsApp charges per conversation depending on country and message type. Typical costs run from a few US cents to around 7 US cents per conversation in MENA. We bill our service as a flat monthly fee plus pass-through Meta charges, with full cost visibility.
