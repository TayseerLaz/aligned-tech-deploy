We design high-performance websites and mobile apps that act as business tools, integrating AI, automation, and SEO to enhance user experience and support long-term growth.

A website or mobile app is no longer just a brochure, for the businesses we work with across MENA, the Gulf and Africa, it is the primary channel customers, partners and employees interact with every day. That is why we build digital products as systems, not as one-off projects. Every site or app we deliver is wired into your CRM, ERP, marketing automation or support stack so the work it does on day one keeps compounding.

Our stack is modern and battle-tested: React, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, Flutter and React Native for cross-platform mobile, with REST and GraphQL APIs backing the front-end. We host on Vercel, AWS, Azure or your own infrastructure depending on cost and compliance. Every project is built mobile-first, accessible (WCAG AA), SEO-ready out of the box, and instrumented with analytics so you see real user behaviour from launch day.

Process matters as much as code. We run a four-phase delivery, discovery, design, build, launch, with weekly demos, written specs and clear hand-off criteria. After launch we offer ongoing support, performance tuning, and feature work on a retainer or per-sprint basis so the product keeps evolving with your business.

Frequently asked questions

Do you design or just build?

Both. Our team includes UX, UI and front-end engineering. We can start from a Figma your team owns, or design the entire experience in-house from research through final UI.

Native or cross-platform mobile?

For most product launches we recommend Flutter or React Native, one codebase, two platforms, and the user experience is now indistinguishable from native for the vast majority of apps. We still build true native (Swift, Kotlin) when performance or platform integrations require it.

How do you handle Arabic, RTL and multilingual sites?

Every site we build is i18n-ready by default. Arabic and RTL are first-class, we handle bidirectional layout, font choice, number formatting and date locales correctly, and we can ship with English, Arabic, French and any other language you need.

What about SEO and performance?

SEO is baked into the build. We ship semantic HTML, schema markup, sitemap, robots, canonical tags, OG and Twitter metadata, and per-page meta titles and descriptions. Sites consistently score 90+ on Lighthouse mobile.

Do you maintain the product after launch?

Yes. We offer post-launch retainers covering monitoring, security patching, feature work, and on-call response for production issues. Most clients stay on retainer indefinitely.
