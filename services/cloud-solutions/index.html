Cloud Solutions

Scalable cloud infrastructure for modern businesses.

We design and manage scalable cloud infrastructures that support modern applications, AI workloads, and distributed teams with strong security and long-term reliability.

Cloud is now the default for most businesses in MENA, the Gulf and Africa, but the path from "we should move to the cloud" to a stable, cost-predictable production environment is rarely linear. We have spent the last decade migrating businesses of all sizes, and the same problems show up over and over: cost overruns from unmonitored services, broken email or VPN during cut-over weekends, and security gaps left open because the migration was rushed.

Our cloud practice is built to remove those problems. We design the target architecture first, Microsoft 365, Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, or hybrid, based on your compliance, latency and cost requirements. We then run the migration in phases that protect production: identity first, then files and email, then workloads, then networking. At every step we keep the existing systems running so a rollback is always one click away.

After cut-over, we tune. Right-sizing virtual machines, identifying idle resources, configuring backup and disaster-recovery policies that match your RTO and RPO targets, and setting up cost alerts so the next month bill never surprises you. For businesses serving customers across multiple countries we also design hybrid setups that keep regulated data inside the country while putting global services on the cloud.

What you can expect

  • Reduce on-premise infrastructure costs by 30-50% within the first year
  • Maintain 99.9% uptime SLAs with automated failover and disaster recovery
  • Cut email and file-server downtime to near-zero during business hours
  • Comply with local data-residency rules across MENA, Gulf and African markets

Frequently asked questions

Will my email and files be down during migration?

No. We use phased cut-over patterns so users keep working throughout. Email is migrated in the background and only flips on the final weekend, with rollback available for at least seven days.

Which cloud should I choose, Microsoft, Azure, AWS or Google?

It depends on your existing tools, compliance needs and team skills. Microsoft 365 and Azure dominate where Office, Teams and AD are already in place. AWS and Google Cloud win on developer-heavy workloads, ML and global reach. We make the recommendation in writing during discovery so the decision is auditable.

How do you handle data residency for MENA and African customers?

We design around the rules that apply in each country, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Qatar, Kenya, South Africa and others all have specific requirements. Where local regions exist we use them; where they do not we build hybrid setups so regulated data stays on-premise.

Can you take over a cloud setup another vendor built?

Yes. We routinely audit and take over Microsoft 365, Azure or AWS environments built by other providers. We start with a free audit covering security, cost, backup posture and licensing, then propose a clean-up plan.

What happens if costs spike?

We configure budgets, anomaly alerts and weekly cost reports. If something spikes, you and we both get notified the same day, and we investigate immediately. Most cost incidents we have seen were caused by misconfigured services that were straightforward to fix.
