Reliable technology foundations for your business.

We provide end-to-end IT services ensuring secure, stable, and high-performance operations, covering support, infrastructure, and maintenance so teams work without disruption.

IT infrastructure is the difference between a workforce that ships and one that loses an hour every morning waiting for systems to come back. For the businesses we serve across MENA, the Gulf and Africa, our managed IT support is the layer that makes everything else possible, email works, files open, VPNs connect, the network never goes down, and someone picks up the phone when something breaks.

We run IT infrastructure end-to-end: design, deployment, day-to-day support and proactive monitoring. That includes the network (switches, routers, firewalls, Wi-Fi), the servers (physical, virtual, cloud), the endpoints (laptops, mobile devices, printers), the identity stack (Active Directory, Azure AD, Intune), and the security layer (antivirus, EDR, patching, backup). We treat the whole thing as one system because it is, a backup that does not run is the same as no backup at all.

Our helpdesk is staffed during business hours with after-hours and 24/7 emergency response available on demand. Tickets are tracked end-to-end so you can see response times, resolution times and trends. We meet quarterly to review what broke, what we fixed proactively and where the next investment should go. The result is an IT environment that gets quieter every quarter instead of louder.

  • Resolve most user tickets within the same business hour

Frequently asked questions

Do you replace our internal IT team?

Not unless you want us to. Most of our clients have us augment a small internal team, we handle infrastructure, monitoring, after-hours support and complex projects, while their internal IT focuses on people and business-specific systems.

How fast do you respond to issues?

During business hours, most tickets get a first response within 15 minutes and resolution within an hour. Critical outages (email down, network down, ransomware) are treated as P1 and worked on continuously until resolved.

Do you support remote and hybrid offices?

Yes. We have built remote and hybrid setups across Lebanon, the Gulf and Africa. Cloud-hosted identity, VPN or zero-trust access, secure laptop provisioning, and remote management are part of our standard stack.

What about cybersecurity?

Security is part of every infrastructure engagement. We deploy EDR, manage patching, run regular vulnerability scans, train staff on phishing, and align with frameworks like ISO 27001 and the GDPR where they apply.

Can you take over support from another provider?

Yes. We onboard new clients with a free audit that documents the current state, identifies gaps, and produces a 90-day stabilisation plan. Most takeovers are seamless to end users.
